# Human Submission Guide: Groww FirstStep

**Note to the Candidate:** This document provides a structured outline to help you write your required 300–700-word submission page for this product case study. 

## 1. The Core Problem & Your Interpretation
*Prompt to reflect on:* Why is Gen Z hesitant to start investing, and how does the concept of "Groww FirstStep" solve this specifically? 
- **Focus on:** Trust, lack of financial context, and the anxiety of losing real money.
- **Key point to mention:** First-time users often don't know *how much* to invest. The product solves this by calculating an estimated investable amount based on income and expenses before even showing them an investment product.

## 2. Product Scope and Deliberate Exclusions
*Prompt to reflect on:* What did you explicitly leave out of the MVP, and why was that a strong product decision?
- **Focus on:** Removing KYC walls, omitting live market tickers, and avoiding real transactions.
- **Key point to mention:** By removing real money from the equation, you lower the barrier to entry, allowing the user to focus entirely on education and comfort-building.

## 3. The Solution & User Journey
*Prompt to reflect on:* How does the user progress through the app, and what is the intended psychological effect?
- **Focus on:** The progression from the "Financial Comfort Check" (Onboarding) -> Dashboard -> SIP Simulator -> Mock Goal Setting.
- **Key point to mention:** The SIP Simulator is designed not to promise returns, but to visualize the habit of compounding. Mention the strict guardrails placed around hypothetical returns.

## 4. Evaluation and Metrics
*Prompt to reflect on:* How would you know if this product concept was successful if it were launched as an A/B test?
- **Focus on:** Education completion rates and the transition rate to the actual Groww investing app.
- **Key point to mention:** A key guardrail metric is ensuring users don't mistake hypothetical simulation returns for guaranteed returns, which is validated through the beginner quiz scores.

## 5. Final Rationale
*Prompt to reflect on:* Why is this the right approach for Groww's business?
- **Focus on:** Long-term Customer Lifetime Value (CLV). By investing in user education upfront, Groww acquires highly intentful, financially disciplined users who are less likely to churn during market downturns.
